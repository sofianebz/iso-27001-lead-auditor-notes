# 02 - Understanding the ISMS

## 1. ISO/IEC 27001:2022

ISO/IEC 27001 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an Information Security Management System (ISMS).

The approach is risk-based and supports the protection of:

- Confidentiality
- Integrity
- Availability

## 2. Structure

The standard contains:

- Clauses 1–3: scope, references and terms
- Clauses 4–10: ISMS requirements
- Annex A: reference set of information security controls

## 3. Clauses 4–10

### Clause 4 - Context of the organization

Covers:
- organizational context
- interested parties
- ISMS scope

### Clause 5 - Leadership

Covers:
- leadership commitment
- information security policy
- roles and responsibilities

### Clause 6 - Planning

Covers:
- risks and opportunities
- information security objectives
- risk assessment
- risk treatment

### Clause 7 - Support

Covers:
- resources
- competence
- awareness
- communication
- documented information

### Clause 8 - Operation

Covers:
- operational planning and control
- information security risk assessment
- implementation of risk treatment

### Clause 9 - Performance evaluation

Covers:
- monitoring and measurement
- internal audit
- management review

### Clause 10 - Improvement

Covers:
- nonconformity
- corrective action
- continual improvement

## 4. Annex A

The 2022 revision reorganized the reference controls into four themes:

- Organizational controls
- People controls
- Physical controls
- Technological controls

The 2022 edition contains 93 controls.

## 5. Auditable evidence

An auditor should evaluate both:

1. The activity or process itself.
2. Objective evidence demonstrating that it is performed as required.

Examples of documented information include:

- ISMS scope
- Statement of Applicability
- risk information
- risk treatment information
- internal audit results
- management review results
