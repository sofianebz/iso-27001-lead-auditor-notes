# 06 - Key Concepts

## PDCA

The management-system approach can be understood through:

- **Plan:** context, objectives, risks and planning
- **Do:** implementation and operation
- **Check:** monitoring, measurement, audits and management review
- **Act:** corrective action and continual improvement

## Risk management flow

```text
Context
   ↓
Risk Assessment
   ↓
Risk Treatment
   ↓
Controls
   ↓
Implementation
   ↓
Monitoring & Audit
   ↓
Residual Risk
   ↓
Continual Improvement
```

## Audit evidence model

```text
Audit Criteria
      ↓
Evidence Collection
      ↓
Evidence Evaluation
      ↓
Audit Findings
      ↓
Audit Conclusion
```

## SoA traceability

```text
Risk
 ↓
Risk Treatment
 ↓
Applicable Control
 ↓
Implementation
 ↓
Objective Evidence
```

## Major vs minor nonconformity

A nonconformity should be classified according to the applicable certification/audit rules and the significance of the failure.

The classification should be supported by objective evidence rather than by the auditor's subjective preference.
