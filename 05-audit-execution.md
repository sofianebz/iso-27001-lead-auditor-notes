# 05  Audit Execution

## 1. Opening meeting

The opening meeting establishes the framework for the audit.

Typical topics:

- audit objectives
- scope
- criteria
- audit team
- timetable
- communication channels
- audit methods
- reporting
- handling of findings
- questions from the auditee

## 2. Collecting audit evidence

Evidence can be collected through:

### Inspection

Review of documents, records, systems or electronic information.

### Observation

Direct observation of activities, processes or environments.

### Interviews

Discussions with relevant personnel.

Interviews should normally be corroborated with other evidence where appropriate.

### Reperformance

Re-performing or testing a process to verify whether it operates as described.

## 3. Evaluating evidence

Audit conclusions should be based on objective evidence.

The auditor compares:

**Audit criteria ↔ Evidence ↔ Actual implementation**

Evidence should be sufficiently relevant and reliable to support the finding.

## 4. Audit findings

Possible findings include:

- conformity
- major nonconformity
- minor nonconformity
- opportunity for improvement
- positive observation

Findings should be communicated to the auditee and supported by objective evidence.

## 5. Closing meeting

The closing meeting presents:

- audit conclusions
- identified findings
- relevant evidence
- next steps
- reporting and corrective-action expectations

## 6. Certification decisions

The certification decision is made by the certification body according to its applicable process and impartiality requirements.

Possible outcomes can include:

- certification
- maintenance of certification
- suspension
- withdrawal
- non-certification

The Lead Auditor's recommendation is not itself the independent certification decision.
